# MIH Bibliography

Canonical bibliography for Modelling Invasive Mosquitoes in Hawaiʻi.

**Filename convention:** `AuthorYear_ShortTitle.pdf`
**Location:** `~/MIH/bibliography/` locally, `bibliography/` on Drive.
**Rule:** No claim enters the manuscript without a PDF in this folder — with
one narrow exception: historical or archival sources (newspaper articles,
pre-1960 government reports, out-of-print texts) can be cited without a PDF
if the citation is bibliographic only and no methodological content is being
drawn from the work. These are marked **📰** below.

## Scope and construction

This bibliography is the union of:

1. References cited in the current `MIH_bibliography.md` (curated 2023–2026)
2. References from Jon Winchester's 2011 MSc thesis (89 references)
3. References from Durrell Kapan et al.'s current draft manuscript
4. References from Winchester & Kapan 2013 (J Am Mosq Control Assoc)
5. Post-2013 Zotero updates covering competitive exclusion, VPD/desiccation,
   satyrization, SDM methods, Hawaii ecology, and climate data sources
6. Proactive methods additions: spThin, ENMeval, ecospat, glmmTMB, DHARMa,
   ERA5-Land, HCDP

**Total:** ~170 entries. Inclusive by design — easier to trim during
manuscript tightening than to hunt down missing citations at submission.

## Categories

### 1. Hawaii mosquito history & dengue
Works establishing invasion timeline, AAEP eradication, dengue outbreaks.

### 2. Species ecology & behavior
Reproductive biology, dispersal, habitat preference, host-seeking.

### 3. Climate & environmental factors
Temperature, humidity, rainfall, elevation, VPD, desiccation tolerance.

### 4. Competition & niche
Competitive exclusion, coexistence mechanisms, resource competition.

### 5. Geographic distribution & SDM
MaxEnt, habitat suitability, range prediction, spatial patterns.

### 6. Disease & vector competence
Dengue, Zika, chikungunya, vector competence, transmission cycles.

### 7. Climate data sources
WorldClim, ERA5-Land, HCDP, GHCN-Daily, Rainfall Atlas.

### 8. Statistical & methods
MaxEnt theory, ENMeval, collinearity, model selection, ZINB regression.
